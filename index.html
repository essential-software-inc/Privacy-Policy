Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026

Note Builder is published by Essential Software. This policy describes what data the app stores, where it lives, and what leaves your device.

On-device storage

All pages, drafts, images, favourites, preferences, and UI state are stored locally on your device. None of this is transmitted to Essential Software.

External images & embeds

Images embedded in a page via URL are fetched directly from their source when the page is built. YouTube embeds load directly from YouTube, and Instagram post embeds load directly from Instagram. Essential Software has no involvement with these third-party hosts.

Linked pages

Tapping a link in a page opens it directly in an in-app viewer, which loads the linked site directly from its own server. Essential Software does not see or store which links you open.

Analytics

Essential Software does not collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports from this app.
